Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Türkçe sürüm aşağıda

App: Crownstack: Royal Army · Publisher: Phantom Game Lab · Contact: phantomgamelab@gmail.com

In short

  • No account, no sign-in, no purchases.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics with Google Firebase Analytics (details below). We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos or any other personal content.
  • No advertising in this version, and we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA / Android Advertising ID) or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

What we collect

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to find crashes and performance problems. Firebase automatically collects:

  • an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase (not linked to your name or Apple ID / Google account);
  • basic usage events, such as the app being opened and screens or game sessions started;
  • device information: model, operating system version, language, app version and screen size;
  • an approximate region (country or city level) derived from your IP address. The IP address itself is not stored in our reports.

The app may also record anonymous performance measurements, such as frame rate, to improve how smoothly the game runs.

How we use it

Only to maintain and improve the game: fixing problems, tuning performance and deciding what to build next. We do not sell this data and do not use it for advertising. Data is processed by Google on our behalf under Google's Firebase terms and is retained for up to 2 months in event-level form.

Data on your device

Game progress and settings are stored locally. Uninstalling the app removes them. We keep no copy on our servers.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal accounts or direct identifiers, we usually cannot link analytics data to a specific person. You can reset or limit collection through your device's privacy settings. For questions or requests under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or Turkish KVKK, email phantomgamelab@gmail.com.

Third parties

Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). If we later add advertising or other services, we will update this policy and the app's privacy disclosures before release.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.

Ne topluyoruz?

Uygulama, oyunun nasıl kullanıldığını anlamak, çökme ve performans sorunlarını bulmak için Google Firebase Analytics kullanır. Firebase otomatik olarak şunları toplar:

  • Firebase'in ürettiği uygulama örneği kimliği (adınla, Apple Kimliğinle ya da Google hesabınla ilişkili değildir);
  • uygulamanın açılması, ekran ve oyun oturumu başlangıçları gibi temel kullanım olayları;
  • cihaz bilgisi: model, işletim sistemi sürümü, dil, uygulama sürümü ve ekran boyutu;
  • IP adresinden türetilen yaklaşık bölge (ülke veya şehir düzeyi). IP adresinin kendisi raporlarımızda saklanmaz.

Oyunun akıcılığını artırmak için kare hızı gibi anonim performans ölçümleri de kaydedilebilir.

Nasıl kullanıyoruz?

Yalnızca oyunu sürdürmek ve geliştirmek için: sorunları gidermek, performansı ayarlamak ve neyi geliştireceğimize karar vermek. Bu veri satılmaz ve reklam için kullanılmaz. Veri, Google'ın Firebase şartlarına göre bizim adımıza Google tarafından işlenir ve olay düzeyinde en fazla 2 ay saklanır.
